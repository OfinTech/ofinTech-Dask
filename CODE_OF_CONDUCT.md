# Code of conduct

OfinTech Desk is built by people from many countries, languages and professions: accountants, engineers,
translators and designers. We want taking part to be a good experience for all of them.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient, in every language. Assume good intent, and ask before you assume.
- Critique the work, not the person. Explain *why* when you disagree, and accept being told *why* in return.
- Welcome newcomers and people asking basic accounting or technical questions.
- Respect privacy: never post anyone's personal data, client data or financial information.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, insults or demeaning comments (including about anyone's nationality, religion, gender, sexuality,
disability, age or appearance); sexual language or imagery; threats; publishing others' private information; and
continuing an interaction after being asked to stop.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions and any project chat, and when you represent the
project elsewhere. Report problems privately to the maintainers through the contact listed on the repository
profile. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and warn, suspend or ban
participants who break these rules.

This code is adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1
(https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

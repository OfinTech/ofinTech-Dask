# Monthly management report

Sections:
1. **Headlines** — 3–5 bullets: revenue, gross margin, operating profit, cash, one notable event.
2. **KPI table** — month, year-to-date, budget, prior year.
3. **Profit and loss** — summarised by section with variances (`variance-analysis`).
4. **Cash** — opening, movements, closing; forecast low point (`cash-flow-forecast`).
5. **Working capital** — DSO, DPO, overdue receivables, upcoming large payments.
6. **Risks and actions** — owner and date for each action.

Practice:
- Figures come from tools; label anything estimated.
- Use the workspace base currency and fiscal year.
- Offer to build or update a dashboard (`create_dashboard` with the profit_and_loss template) and a monthly workflow that drafts this pack.

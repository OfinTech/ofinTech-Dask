# Revenue recognition (IFRS 15 / ASC 606)

This is general technical guidance, not an audit opinion; complex contracts should be reviewed by the user's auditor or adviser.

The five steps:
1. **Identify the contract** — approved, rights and payment terms identifiable, commercial substance, collection probable.
2. **Identify performance obligations** — distinct goods/services (capable of being distinct and distinct in context). Typical: licence, implementation, support, hardware.
3. **Determine the transaction price** — fixed and variable consideration (discounts, rebates, bonuses) with the constraint; significant financing components; non-cash consideration; amounts payable to the customer.
4. **Allocate** — by relative stand-alone selling prices (observable, or adjusted market / expected cost plus margin / residual where allowed).
5. **Recognise** — over time if one of the criteria is met (customer consumes as performed; customer controls asset as created; no alternative use + enforceable right to payment), otherwise at a point in time when control transfers.

Also cover where relevant: contract costs (incremental costs to obtain; costs to fulfil), principal vs agent, licences (right to access vs right to use), contract modifications, disclosures.

Answer with: the steps applied to the user's contract, a recognition schedule (table), journal entries, and the judgements that need sign-off. Note differences between IFRS and US GAAP where they matter (e.g. collectability threshold, some licence and shipping guidance).

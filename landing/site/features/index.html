Features

From the general ledger to the decision, with every figure traced.

OfinTech Desk reads your ledger and turns it into the numbers a finance lead acts on: 35 KPIs against your own targets, forecasts you approve, and cost centres that follow a project through the journal and the bank. Every value opens up to the accounts and lines behind it.

Financial analysis

35 KPIs on your targets, not a benchmark

Growth and profitability, liquidity, working capital, leverage, cash flow, and cost and control, computed in code from the general ledger. The finance lead sets a target and a warning level for each one, and the card shows on target, watch or act.

  • Any chart of accounts. Each account gets a role, such as cash, debt, payroll or depreciation, suggested from its name in English, Arabic, Spanish or French and confirmed by a person.
  • Formulas you can read and change. DSO is receivables / SUMN(revenue, 3) * 91.25. Edit any formula or add KPIs of your own.
  • Open any value to see its 24-month history, the measures in the formula and the ledger accounts behind them.
  • Real balances. Month-end balances are rebuilt from the synced balance sheet, and cash flow is split into operating, investing and financing.

Growth and profitability

Revenue growth, gross, EBITDA, operating and net margins, return on equity, assets and capital employed.

Liquidity

Current, quick and cash ratios, working capital, days of cash on hand.

Working capital

DSO, DPO, DIO, cash conversion cycle, overdue receivables and payables, collection rate.

Leverage

Debt to equity, net debt to EBITDA, interest cover, debt service coverage, equity ratio.

Cash flow

Operating and free cash flow, cash runway, cash conversion of profit.

Cost and control

Opex and payroll to revenue, margin of safety, revenue against plan, forecast accuracy, customer concentration.

Forecasting

Your assumptions, our arithmetic, the CFO's approval

Choose a method for every P&L line: growth, same month last year, a percentage of another line, an average, a trend, a fixed amount or month by month. Add receivable, inventory and payable days, capital spending and debt repayment. The forecast projects profit, working capital and cash, and names the month cash would run out.

  • Scenarios and versions, compared side by side.
  • Draft → submitted → approved. Only the finance lead approves or makes a forecast the plan, and approved figures are frozen.
  • The plan feeds the revenue against plan and forecast accuracy KPIs, and each approved forecast shows its error and bias as months close.
  • A statistical forecast (Holt or Holt-Winters) is drawn next to yours, with its measured error, as a check. It never replaces your assumptions.

Cost centres

Follow a project through the ledger, the journal and the bank

  • Rules instead of a ledger field. Combine tags, accounts, suppliers, words and dates, so it works on books that were never tagged. The untagged share shows how much was picked up by a rule rather than a tag.
  • Cost against cash. See what was paid directly, which supplier bills are paid or still unpaid, and which bank payments may belong to the project.
  • The date the budget runs out at the current pace, overall and for each budget line.
  • Budgets from your own file. Read from the Excel or Word file the team already uses; every line keeps the cell it came from.

The workspace

Everything around the numbers

An assistant that cites

Ask about your numbers in any of four languages. Answers name the documents, KPIs and ledger lines they used, and any figure without a source is flagged.

Evidence-linked files

Workbooks and reports where inputs drill down to their rows and totals are live formulas. Your own Excel and Word files can be filled in too, keeping their layout.

Approvals and audit

Nothing is sent or changed without a person's approval, and every decision is logged.

Workflows

Month-end packs, collections and bills due, on a schedule, from an e-mail or from another system's webhook.

Any ledger, any model

QuickBooks Online, Xero, Zoho Books, Odoo, or any system through the open accounting API. Any model provider, cloud or local.

On your infrastructure

A desktop app for each person and an optional Team Server you run yourself. Open source under Apache-2.0.

Plainly stated

  • KPIs and forecasts are management figures computed from your ledger. They are not audited statements and not financial advice.
  • There are no industry benchmarks. Comparisons are against your own targets and history.
  • Cash comes from the ledger's bank lines; there is no live bank connection. Connectors read data and never post entries.

## Behaviour changes shipped in this phase (for operators)

- Team Server sessions end after 30 minutes of inactivity (was 60) and after 30 days (was never); configurable
  `DESK_SESSION_IDLE_MINUTES` 30–45, `DESK_SESSION_DAYS`.
- Pending approvals expire (4 h / 24 h); old pending rows ask again.
- A viewer's assistant no longer creates dashboards, files, close runs or refreshes accounting data.
- Migration 19 runs automatically on start (additive: new columns, an index, two triggers). Encrypted backups made
  before it restore and migrate on first start.
- The desktop app locks its screen after the inactivity window and when the computer locks or sleeps.

## Remaining risks (full register in `OPEN-RISKS.md`)

Accepted (owner sign-off still pending): AR-1…AR-10. Open: OP-3 (desktop retention), OP-7 (dev-tree
`minimatch`), OP-8 (test-suite stall watch — every run since `4068d0d` clean, 6 of the 10 needed), OP-11 (hash
pinning), OP-12 (three page behaviours of the session policy not browser-automated), OP-13 (audit log in the
application database; external append-only shipping recommended for regulated deployments). Limitations
documented rather than fixed: approvals are user-bound, not session-bound; the desktop lock is a screen lock, not
a re-authentication; the model in the live run is simulated (AR-3).

## FINAL DECISION

**APPROVED WITH EXPLICIT LIMITATIONS**

Every acceptance criterion above is met with executed evidence on the exact release commit, in-process and over
real HTTP against a real server process; no critical or high finding remains open; one path found during this
phase (a viewer's assistant creating workspace records) was closed and proven before the gate. The limitations
that keep this from an unqualified approval are explicit and owned: the ten accepted risks await the owner's
recorded sign-off, live third-party integrations and a real model are not part of the evidence, and the six open
items above are low-severity with dates. Security is enforced by the application — roles from the membership
table, approvals re-validated by the engine at execution, sessions ended by the server — not by trusting the
model, the page or the user.
